## What's new

**Longer battery life**
- The frame limiter sleeps until the next frame is due. Before, its pacing thread woke every half millisecond and kept a CPU core busy for the whole session.
- During play the display runs at the game's 60 FPS cap, typically 60 Hz instead of 120 Hz. On Android 15 and later the launcher also turns off the boost to the maximum rate that every touch would trigger.
- While the app is in the background, WoW is limited to 10 FPS and stays connected to Battle.net. The previous limit returns when you come back.
- **More controls → Battery saver (30 FPS)** is the new name for the 30 FPS limit.

**Controls**
- The movement stick starts anywhere in the lower-left corner and its base follows your thumb. A thumb that lands just beside the stick no longer clicks into the game world.
- Jump, Target and Interact respond as soon as you touch them, light up while pressed and give a short haptic tick. Hold Jump to keep rising while swimming or flying.
- Larger chat buttons beside the minimap.

**Also included:** the built-in keyboard and the fullscreen chat composer from the .32 and .33 previews.

## Installing next to .26–.33

.34 uses a new signing key and its own app IDs (`app.wowforever.android` and `app.wowforever.android.pixel`), so Android installs it as a separate app next to your current launcher. Select your existing WoW folder with **Find existing game files**. Your skill arcs, chat size and WoW settings are stored there. Uninstall the old launcher once .34 works. Later updates in this line install over .34. [Step-by-step instructions](https://github.com/bthginfo/wow-forever-mobile#move-to-34)
